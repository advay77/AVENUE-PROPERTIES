Commercial services

Three ways we work on commercial space

Whether you are taking space, giving space or investing in it, the work is different each time.

Taking space

Headcount to floor plate, lease terms, lock-in, escalation and the fit-out budget — sized before you sign, not after.

Leasing out

Tenant search, credit sense-check, rent benchmarking for the building, and a lease that holds up when the tenant changes.

Investing

Yield maths on a tenanted unit, exit liquidity in that micro-market, and the title and approval checks before money moves.

Fit-out

Take the floor, then make it work

Most commercial deals stall after signing, when the fit-out turns out to cost more than the first year of rent. We plan the two together.

Layout before lease

A workstation layout drawn on the actual floor plate, so you know the real seat count before signing.

Budget and timeline

A fit-out number you can take to a board, and a handover date you can plan a move around.

Execution

Civil, electrical, HVAC, furniture and signage coordinated through one point of contact.
