What we do

Residential and commercial interiors

Full fit-out or a single room. The scope is yours to set — what we hold is the drawing, the budget and the site.

Residential interiors

False ceilings, flooring, painting, lighting, wardrobes and furniture — room by room or the whole home in one go.

Office & commercial fit-outs

Workstations, cabins, reception, conference rooms and pantry, planned around headcount and the lease term.

Modular kitchens & wardrobes

Measured on site, made to the drawing, installed and finished — the two things that decide how a home feels to live in.

Lighting & electricals

Light layouts that suit how the room is actually used, with the switching and points planned before the walls close.

Renovation & repair

Older flats and floors brought up to date — bathrooms, kitchens, flooring and a full repaint.

Rental-ready packages

The minimum fit-out that lifts a rental into a higher bracket, costed against the extra rent it earns.

How it runs

From the first measurement to the handover

Four stages, each with something you can see and sign off before the next begins.

  1. Site measurement

    We measure the actual flat or floor — builder drawings and reality are rarely the same.

  2. Design & estimate

    Layouts, material choices and a line-by-line estimate, so there are no surprises mid-project.

  3. Execution

    Civil, carpentry, electrical and painting run in sequence, with one point of contact throughout.

  4. Handover

    Snag list closed, cleaning done, warranties and care instructions handed across.
