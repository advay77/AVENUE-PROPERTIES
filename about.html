Who we are

We work one sector at a time

Noida is not one market. Sector 50 behaves nothing like Sector 137, and Greater Noida West is a different conversation again.

Avenue Properties is a consultancy. That means the first conversation is about your budget, your commute and when you actually need to move — not about whichever unit we are keenest to close this week.

We cover residential buying and selling, rentals, and commercial sale, rental and leasing. Alongside that we handle the parts most people find hardest to do alone: valuing a property honestly, negotiating on price, and keeping the documentation moving so a deal does not stall at the registry.

We also run an interior services arm, so a flat or office can be fitted out by the same team that handed you the keys.

Services

What we actually do for you

Six pieces of work. Most clients need four or five of them on any one transaction.

Expert property consultation

A shortlist built from your budget, family needs and commute — not whatever is easiest to sell this week.

Site visit assistance

Visits planned sector by sector so you see several comparable options in one trip, with someone who knows the block.

Price negotiation

We quote the sector’s recent transaction range before you make an offer, and carry the negotiation for you.

Property valuation

An honest view of what your flat, floor or shop should fetch today, based on what comparable units actually closed at.

Documentation coordination

Agreement to sale, registry appointment, society NOC and handover — coordinated so nothing stalls at the last step.

Residential & commercial

Flats, builder floors and plots on one side; offices, retail and leasing on the other. Same consultant either way.
